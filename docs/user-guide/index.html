The example

Larkspur Mutual claims platform replacement

Larkspur Mutual, a fictional general insurer, is replacing its ClaimsOne mainframe claims system. The new platform covers first notification of loss, triage, reserving, settlement and member self-service, with integrations to the policy mainframe, Dynamics 365 finance and SEPA bank payments, and migration of 1.4 million closed claims. The walkthrough shows this bid after it has been through every stage. Where a stage is easier to understand mid-flight, it also shows a second, unfinished bid, Aldermoor Health Trust's patient and referral portal.

Key terms

The words the app uses

Opportunity
One bid for one customer.
Source
Anything the customer sent or said: an email, transcript, document, note or answer. Stored unchanged.
Evidence
The exact passage in a Source that supports a Requirement or Finding.
Requirement
One thing the customer needs, with its category and Evidence. People confirm it.
Gap
Information the estimate needs that the Sources don't give.
Clarification Question
The question that closes a Gap, approved and sent to the customer.
Condition
An Assumption the customer must make true: a decision or something they provide. No hours.
Contingency
An Assumption for something nobody can know yet, priced as an allowance in hours.
Assessment
A specialist agent's verdict, effort and reasoning for the confirmed scope.
Finding
A problem an agent raises, ranked by severity. Critical Findings block submission.
Conflict
Two statements or sizings that can't both be true.
Baseline
The approved Estimate version that delivery is measured against.
Actuals
Hours actually spent per Estimate line, with the cause of any variance.
Trace
The append-only record of every action, by people and agents.

Start here

Welcome

The Pre Sales Agent Framework helps a presales team answer a tender. You give it everything the customer sent. Agents extract the requirements, find what's missing, assess feasibility and effort, and draft an estimate. People confirm, decide and approve at every step, and every number can be traced back to the sentence it came from.

How to

  1. Sign in with your company account (Auth0). Your roles decide what you can see; Opportunity access comes from being its owner or a collaborator.
  2. Use the sidebar or press Ctrl K to open the command palette.
  3. The toggle at the bottom of the sidebar switches light and dark theme; [ collapses the sidebar and ? lists keyboard shortcuts.

Step 1

Prepare your knowledge

Knowledge is set up once by an administrator and reused on every bid. Agents cite it, Checklists turn it into mandatory questions, and Coverage shows where it is thin.

How to

  1. Knowledge > Sources > Register Source: upload a delivery guide or playbook and tag it with products and integration types. It is chunked and indexed for search.
  2. Knowledge > Checklists > New Checklist: add items for an integration type, mark the must-ask ones as Mandatory, then publish a version.
  3. Knowledge > Coverage: check each product and integration type for Ready, Partial or Missing coverage.
  4. Knowledge > Graph: search and explore how products, integration types, sources and checklists connect.

Step 2

Create the opportunity

An Opportunity is one bid for one customer. It holds every Source, Requirement, Gap, Assessment and Estimate version for that bid.

How to

  1. Click New Opportunity (or press c with single-key shortcuts on).
  2. Click Import from file and choose the customer's email, transcript or document. Fields marked From file were filled by the Opportunity Intake Agent, with the quote it used.
  3. Check the customer name, title, products in scope (one per line) and industry, and set the target proposal date.
  4. Click Create Opportunity. The imported file becomes Source 1. Add collaborators from the Opportunity header.

Step 3

Follow progress on the Overview

The Overview answers one question: what stands between this bid and submission?

How to

  1. Read the four tiles: Requirements confirmed, Open Gaps, Open Conflicts and the current Estimate.
  2. Follow the Submission pipeline: eight stages from Sources added to Review and Baseline.
  3. Work through Needs you, which lists Findings, Gaps and Assumptions waiting for you, most severe first.
  4. Use Submission blockers to jump straight to whatever stops Submit.

Step 4

Add sources

Sources are the customer's own words. They are stored unchanged and versioned, and every Requirement and Finding cites them.

How to

  1. Open the Sources tab and drop files, or click Choose files. Supported formats: .eml, .msg, .txt, .docx, .pdf and .vtt (Teams transcripts), up to 50 MB each.
  2. Click Paste text for a chat message or anything else without a file.
  3. Wait until every Source shows Parsed before opening Requirements, so extraction sees all of them.
  4. Select a Source to open the Inspector: metadata, Download original, and the extracted text.

Step 5

Confirm requirements

Requirements are extracted automatically, but only people confirm them. Confirmed Requirements are locked: later Sources propose changes instead of overwriting them.

How to

  1. Filter by Category or State, and open a Requirement to see its evidence highlighted in the Source.
  2. Use Edit, Split or Merge to fix the wording or granularity; change the classification if needed.
  3. Confirm each Requirement. Add requirement adds one by hand.
  4. When a new Source changes a confirmed Requirement, accept or reject the pending change.
  5. Text in a Source that tries to instruct the AI is treated as content and flagged, never followed.

Step 6

Close the gaps

A Gap is something the estimate needs that the Sources don't say. Each Gap gets a Clarification Question, and every Gap ends in one of four ways: answered, converted to an Assumption (a Condition or a Contingency), converted to a Risk, or dismissed with a reason.

How to

  1. Set the Integration Types on integration Requirements (the yellow banner shows any that are missing) so the right Checklists apply.
  2. Review the Gaps, ranked by impact. Filter by Impact, Area or Question status.
  3. Edit, merge or drop the drafted questions, then Approve (or Approve all), export them and mark them Sent.
  4. When the customer replies, record the answer on the question. Confirm or reject any Possible answer suggestions on other questions.
  5. Convert what the customer can't know yet into a Contingency (priced in hours), and what they must decide or provide into a Condition.

Step 7

Run the assessments

An assessment run asks specialist agents to judge the confirmed scope. They propose; you decide.

How to

  1. Open Assessments and click Start new run. Watch each agent's progress and duration; pause, resume or cancel a task if needed.
  2. Read each agent's verdict, confidence, effort and cited Requirements in Results so far.
  3. Work through Findings, ranked by severity. Resolve each critical Finding, or override it with a reason.
  4. Disagree with an agent? Click Challenge, give the new information, confirm the reassessment and a new Estimate version follows.

Step 8

Resolve conflicts

A Conflict is two things that can't both be true: two dates, two volumes, or two agents sizing the same Requirement differently. Open Conflicts block submission.

How to

  1. Open Conflicts and select an open Conflict.
  2. Choose one of the positions shown, or A different position.
  3. Give a reason (required) and click Resolve. The decision and reason are kept in the resolved list and the Trace.

Step 9

Build and submit the estimate

The Estimate is built from the Assessments, line by line. Every line covers named Requirements, and every Assumption and Risk comes from a Gap.

How to

  1. Review the draft lines: covered Requirements, role mix (Engineer, PM, QA), effort, contingency and total hours. Edit a line in place, with a reason.
  2. Accept each Assumption in the Assumptions Register and check the Contingency hours and Risks.
  3. Clear the Submission blockers, then Submit. Submitted versions can't be edited; New version starts the next one.
  4. Send a Review request to a named reviewer. Once approved, set the Baseline.
  5. Use Diff vs and All versions to compare, and Export the Estimate, Assumptions Register and questions to Excel or Word.

Step 10

Review in the Inbox

The Inbox holds your review requests and notifications.

How to

  1. Select an item to open it beside the list.
  2. As a reviewer, open the Estimate, then approve it or send it back with comments.
  3. Use Mark all read to clear the list.

Step 11

Track actuals and trace decisions

After delivery, actual hours close the loop: they show which estimates and which Assumptions held up.

How to

  1. On Actuals, enter actual hours per line and pick a variance cause; name the failed Assumption on an overrun.
  2. Or click Import from file: Download template, fill it in, Choose file, review the preview and Confirm.
  3. Click Save Actuals. Reports then shows estimate against actual across Opportunities, with a drill-down from an overrun to its failed Assumption (Head of Delivery and platform administrators).
  4. Open Trace and filter by Subject, Actor or Event to audit any decision.

Step 12

Administration

Admin is for platform administrators. Roles themselves are managed in Auth0.

How to

  1. Users and roles: see who has which role; Invite user opens Auth0.
  2. Catalogue: add Integration Types and Work Packages (New entry).
  3. Agents: the registry of agents, versions, models and tool permissions, plus Denied tool calls.
  4. Evals: run reference sets and check release bars before accepting a new agent configuration.
  5. Settings (everyone): theme, row density and single-key shortcuts, saved in this browser.

Reference

Full feature list

Everything the application does today, grouped by area.

Opportunities

  • Opportunity list with derived status, open items and current Estimate
  • Views: All, Mine, Due in 14 days; filters by status, owner, product and proposal date
  • Create by hand or Import from file (email, transcript, document) with AI-suggested fields
  • Owner and collaborators; sales representatives see a restricted view
  • Overview tiles, eight-stage submission pipeline, Needs you queue, submission blockers, recent activity
  • Record the outcome (won or lost), contract end date and retention; reopen with a reason

Sources and intake

  • Upload .eml, .msg, .txt, .docx, .pdf and .vtt, up to 50 MB each, or paste text
  • Sources stored unchanged and versioned; download the original
  • Source Inspector with extracted text and activity
  • Customer answers recorded as Sources
  • Prompt-injection lines in customer content flagged and ignored

Requirements

  • Automatic extraction with cited Evidence (source and passage)
  • Categories: functional, integration, data, security, non-functional, commercial
  • Edit, split, merge, reclassify, add, delete and confirm
  • Confirmed Requirements locked; incremental intake raises pending changes to accept or reject
  • Integration Type tagging so Checklists apply

Gaps and clarification

  • Model-detected Gaps plus deterministic Gaps from mandatory Checklist items
  • Ranked by impact, filtered by impact, area and question status
  • Drafted Clarification Questions: edit, merge, drop, approve, approve all, export, mark sent
  • Record answers; Possible answer suggestions across other sent questions
  • Convert to Condition, Contingency or Risk, or dismiss with a reason
  • Banners for missing Integration Types and missing knowledge coverage

Assessments

  • Engineering, PM, Security, Research and Critic agents plus a Red Team
  • Live agent run panel with progress, duration, pause, resume, cancel and retry
  • Verdict, confidence, effort and cited Requirements per agent
  • Findings table by severity; resolve or override with a reason
  • Challenges that trigger reassessment and a new Estimate version

Conflicts

  • Contradictions between Sources (dates, volumes) and between agents (sizing)
  • Choose a position or a different one, with a required reason
  • Open Conflicts block submission; resolved list with who decided

Estimate

  • Lines built from Assessments, covering named Requirements, with role mix and hours
  • Edit lines in place with a reason; every edit traced
  • Assumptions Register: Conditions and Contingencies (with hours), each from a Gap, each accepted by a named person
  • Risks register
  • Submission blockers gate; version lifecycle Draft, In review, Approved, Baseline
  • Review requests to named reviewers; decisions recorded
  • New version, All versions, Diff against any version
  • Export to Excel (.xlsx) and Word (.docx)

Actuals and Reports

  • Actual hours per Estimate line with a variance cause and failed Assumption
  • Import from an Excel or CSV template with preview and confirm
  • Reports: estimate against actual, pipeline and cost, with drill-down to failed Assumptions

Knowledge

  • Knowledge Sources tagged by product and integration type, with review dates and stale flag
  • Checklists with mandatory items, versioned and published
  • Coverage matrix by product and integration type
  • Knowledge Graph with search and coverage states
  • Research Agent cites Knowledge Sources in Assessments

Trust and audit

  • Append-only Decision Trace for every human and agent action
  • Filter by subject, actor and event; trace a number to its Evidence
  • Agent Registry with per-agent tool permissions; denied calls blocked and audited
  • Evals with release bars (extraction recall, citation validity, gap recall, evidence support)

Workspace

  • Inbox for review requests and notifications
  • Command palette (Ctrl K), keyboard shortcuts (?), collapsible sidebar
  • Light, dark or system theme; comfortable or compact density; optional single-key shortcuts
  • Roles from Auth0: presales engineer, delivery manager, engineering reviewer, Head of Delivery, sales representative, platform administrator
