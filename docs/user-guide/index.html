The example

Larkspur Mutual claims platform replacement

Larkspur Mutual, a fictional general insurer, is replacing its ClaimsOne mainframe claims system. The new platform covers first notification of loss, triage, reserving, settlement and member self-service, with integrations to the policy mainframe, Dynamics 365 finance and SEPA bank payments, and migration of 1.4 million closed claims. The walkthrough starts from an empty workspace and takes this bid through all eight stages of the submission pipeline, then records actuals and the outcome. Every screenshot comes from that one run.

Key terms

The words the app uses

Opportunity
One bid for one customer.
Source
Anything the customer sent or said: an email, transcript, document, note or answer. Stored unchanged.
Evidence
The exact passage in a Source that supports a Requirement or Finding.
Requirement
One thing the customer needs, with its category and Evidence. People confirm it.
Gap
Information the estimate needs that the Sources don't give.
Clarification Question
The question that closes a Gap, approved and sent to the customer.
Condition
An Assumption the customer must make true: a decision or something they provide. No hours.
Contingency
An Assumption for something nobody can know yet, priced as an allowance in hours.
Assessment
A specialist agent's verdict, effort and reasoning for the confirmed scope.
Finding
A problem an agent raises, ranked by severity. Critical Findings block submission.
Conflict
Two statements or sizings that can't both be true.
Baseline
The approved Estimate version that delivery is measured against.
Actuals
Hours actually spent per Estimate line, with the cause of any variance.
Trace
The append-only record of every action, by people and agents.

Start here

Welcome

The Pre Sales Agent Framework helps a presales team answer a tender. You give it everything the customer sent. Agents extract the requirements, find what's missing, assess feasibility and effort, and draft an estimate. People confirm, decide and approve at every step, and every number can be traced back to the sentence it came from.

How to

  1. Sign in with your company account (Auth0). Your roles decide what you can see; Opportunity access comes from being its owner, a collaborator or a reviewer.
  2. Use the sidebar or press Ctrl K to open the command palette.
  3. The toggle at the bottom of the sidebar switches light and dark theme; [ collapses the sidebar and ? lists keyboard shortcuts.

Step 1

Prepare your knowledge

Knowledge is set up once by an administrator and reused on every bid. Agents cite it, Checklists turn it into must-ask questions, and Coverage shows where it is thin.

How to

  1. Admin > Catalogue > New entry: add each Integration Type and Work Package with a code, name and definition.
  2. Knowledge > Sources > Register Source: upload a delivery guide or playbook, give it a title and product, and tick the Integration Types it covers. Wait for Ready; if it shows Embedding failed, check the embedding model server is running and click Retry.
  3. Knowledge > Checklists > New Checklist: name it, choose a domain and scope (products or Integration Types), add items with an impact and a reason, tick Mandatory on the must-ask ones, then Publish.
  4. Knowledge > Coverage and Graph: check each product and Integration Type for Ready, Partial or Missing coverage.

Step 2

Create the opportunity

An Opportunity is one bid for one customer. It holds every Source, Requirement, Gap, Assessment and Estimate version for that bid, and its Overview tracks eight stages from Sources added to Review and Baseline.

How to

  1. Click New Opportunity (or press c with single-key shortcuts on).
  2. Click Import from file and choose the customer's email, transcript or document. Fields marked From file were filled by the Opportunity Intake Agent.
  3. Check the customer name, title, products in scope (one per line), industry and target proposal date.
  4. Click Create Opportunity. The imported file becomes Source 1 and the Overview opens.

Step 3

Add sources

Sources are the customer's own words. They are stored unchanged and versioned, and every Requirement and Finding cites them. Stage 1 of the pipeline is complete when every Source is parsed.

How to

  1. Open the Sources tab and drop files, or click Choose files: .eml, .msg, .txt, .docx, .pdf and .vtt (Teams transcripts), up to 50 MB each.
  2. Click Paste text for a chat message or anything else without a file, then Add.
  3. Wait until every Source shows Parsed.
  4. Select a Source to open the Inspector: metadata, Download original, and the extracted text.
  5. Text that tries to instruct the AI (for example "ignore previous instructions") is treated as content and never followed.

Step 4

Confirm requirements

The Intake Agent extracts Requirements automatically, but only people confirm them. Confirmed Requirements are locked: later Sources propose changes instead of overwriting them. Stages 2 and 3 cover extraction and confirmation.

How to

  1. Filter by Category or State, and open a Requirement to see its Evidence highlighted in the Source.
  2. Use Edit, Split or Merge to fix wording or granularity. To merge, select each Requirement for merge, click Merge (n), write the merged text and click Merge.
  3. Tick the Integration Types on each integration Requirement in the Inspector; the Gaps page warns about any without one.
  4. Click Confirm all (or Confirm on each).
  5. When a new Source changes a confirmed Requirement, a pending changes panel appears. Click Review, then Accept or Reject, and confirm again.
  6. If the banner says Assessments used Requirements that have changed, re-run them from the Assessments tab.

Step 5

Close the gaps

A Gap is something the estimate needs that the Sources don't say. Each Gap gets a Clarification Question. Every Gap ends in one of four ways: answered, converted to an Assumption (a Condition or a Contingency), converted to a Risk, or dismissed with a reason. Stage 4 is complete when none is open.

How to

  1. Review the Gaps, ranked by impact. Filter by Impact, Area or Question status. Mandatory Checklist Gaps are marked Checklist and Mandatory.
  2. Open a Gap and use Edit question to sharpen the draft, then Approve question or Approve all.
  3. Export questions as Word, an email draft (.eml) or copied text, then Mark all sent with the date.
  4. When the customer replies, open the question, click Record answer, paste the answer and record it. The answer becomes a Source.
  5. Where the app shows Possible answer in Source … on another question, click Confirm or Not an answer.
  6. Gaps the customer can't answer yet become Contingencies, and decisions they own become Conditions, when you accept the Estimate's Assumptions (step 8). Use Convert to Risk or Dismiss for the rest.

Step 6

Run the assessments

Specialist agents judge the confirmed scope. They propose; you decide. Runs start automatically when Requirements and Gaps change, and you can start one yourself. Stage 5 is complete when no critical Finding is open.

How to

  1. Open Assessments. The agent run panel shows each agent's progress and time; Cancel run or Start new run from there.
  2. Read each agent's verdict, confidence, effort and cited Requirements in Results so far.
  3. Disagree? Click Challenge on an agent's result, give the reason (required), choose the Assessments to rerun and click Rerun. A new Estimate version follows.
  4. Work through Findings, ranked by severity. For each critical Finding choose Supply Evidence, Mark resolved or Override, with a reason.

Step 7

Resolve conflicts

A Conflict is two things that can't both be true: two dates, two volumes, or two agents sizing the same Requirement differently. Stage 6 is complete when none is open.

How to

  1. Open Conflicts; the first open Conflict is selected.
  2. Choose one of the positions shown, or A different position.
  3. Give a reason (required) and click Resolve. The decision and reason are kept in the resolved list and the Trace.

Step 8

Build and submit the estimate

The Estimate is built from the Assessments, line by line. Every line covers named Requirements, and every Assumption and Risk comes from a Gap. Stage 7 is complete when every Assumption is accepted.

How to

  1. Review the lines: covered Requirements, role mix (Engineer, PM, QA), effort, contingency and total hours.
  2. To change a line, click its title, set Effort (h) or the role mix, give a Reason for the change and Save. The line is marked Edited.
  3. In the Assumptions Register, check the Conditions and Contingencies (with hours), then Accept each or Accept all. Accepting converts the Gaps they came from.
  4. When the Overview shows no blockers, click Submit and confirm. Submitted versions can't be edited; New version starts the next one.
  5. Use Diff vs, All versions and Export (Excel or Word).

Step 9

Review and baseline

Stage 8: a named reviewer approves the submitted version, and the owner sets it as the Baseline that delivery is measured against.

How to

  1. Share the Opportunity: on the Overview, type a name or email under Add collaborator and click Add.
  2. On the Estimate, click Request review, choose the review type (Engineering, PM or Security), choose reviewers, add a message and click Send.
  3. The reviewer opens it from their Inbox and chooses Approve, Reject, Challenge, Request evidence, Request alternative or Escalate. The owner is notified in their Inbox.
  4. Once approved, click Set Baseline and confirm. A Baseline is replaced only by a newer approved version.
  5. Open Trace and filter by Subject, Actor or Event to audit any decision.

Step 10

Track actuals and close the bid

After delivery, actual hours close the loop: they show which estimates, and which Assumptions, held up. The outcome then sets how long the data is kept.

How to

  1. On Actuals, click Import from file, then Download template. Fill in Actual h and a variance cause per line, naming the failed Assumption on an overrun.
  2. Click Choose file, check the preview (to create, to correct, invalid) and click Confirm. Or enter hours by line and click Save Actuals.
  3. On the Overview, click Mark as won or Close as lost, give a reason and confirm. The Outcome card shows the retention date; Reopen undoes it with a reason.

Step 11

Reports

Reports compare estimates with actuals across every Opportunity you can read. They are for the Head of Delivery and platform administrators.

How to

  1. Open Reports > Estimate vs actual. The tiles show closed Opportunities, total variance and failed Assumptions.
  2. Choose Group by: Integration Type, Opportunity, Product, Work Package or Cause. Filter by product, work package, integration type, cause and closed dates.
  3. Expand a row to see the Opportunities behind it, and click its failed Assumptions to see which ones failed, with a link to the Trace.
  4. Pipeline and Cost and usage are not available yet.

Step 12

Administration

Admin is for platform administrators. Roles themselves are managed in Auth0.

How to

  1. Users and roles: who has which role; Invite user opens Auth0.
  2. Agents: the registry of agents, versions, models and tool permissions, plus Denied tool calls.
  3. Evals: run reference sets and check release bars before accepting a new agent configuration.
  4. Retention: Opportunities due for purge in the next 30 days and the last 20 purges. Purging is automatic after the retention date.
  5. Settings (everyone): theme, row density and single-key shortcuts, saved in this browser.

Reference

Full feature list

Everything the application does today, grouped by area.

Opportunities

  • Opportunity list with derived status, open items and current Estimate
  • Views: All, Mine, Due in 14 days; filters by status, owner, product and proposal date
  • Create by hand or Import from file (email, transcript, document) with AI-filled fields
  • Owner, collaborators and reviewers; sales representatives see a restricted view
  • Overview: tiles, eight-stage submission pipeline, Needs you queue, submission blockers, recent activity, latest agent decision
  • Outcome: won or lost with a reason, retention date, reopen with a reason

Sources and intake

  • Upload .eml, .msg, .txt, .docx, .pdf and .vtt, up to 50 MB each, or paste text
  • Sources stored unchanged and versioned; download the original
  • Source Inspector with extracted text and activity
  • Customer answers recorded as Sources
  • Prompt-injection text in customer content treated as content and flagged

Requirements

  • Automatic extraction with cited Evidence (source and passage)
  • Categories: functional, integration, data, security, non-functional, commercial
  • Edit, split, merge, reclassify, add, delete, confirm and confirm all
  • Integration Type tagging so Checklists apply
  • Locked once confirmed; incremental intake raises pending changes to accept or reject
  • Warning when Assessments used Requirements that have since changed

Gaps and clarification

  • Model-detected Gaps plus mandatory Gaps from Checklist items
  • Ranked by impact; filters by impact, area and question status
  • Drafted Clarification Questions: edit, approve, approve all, export (Word, .eml, copy), mark sent
  • Record answers; Possible answer suggestions on other sent questions to confirm or reject
  • Convert to Condition or Contingency (via Assumptions), convert to Risk, or dismiss with a reason
  • Banners for missing Integration Types and missing knowledge coverage

Assessments

  • Engineering, PM, Security and Research agents, a Critic and a Red Team
  • Live agent run panel with progress, duration, cancel and new run
  • Verdict, confidence, effort and cited Requirements per agent
  • Findings by severity; supply evidence, mark resolved or override with a reason
  • Challenges that rerun chosen Assessments and produce a new Estimate version

Conflicts

  • Contradictions between Sources and between agents' sizing
  • Choose a position or a different one, with a required reason
  • Open Conflicts block submission; resolved list with who decided

Estimate

  • Lines built from Assessments, covering named Requirements, with role mix and hours
  • Edit lines in place with a reason; every edit traced and marked Edited
  • Assumptions Register: Conditions and Contingencies (with hours), each from a Gap, accepted by a named person
  • Risks register
  • Submission blockers; version lifecycle Draft, In review, Approved, Baseline
  • Review requests by type (Engineering, PM, Security) to named reviewers
  • New version, All versions, Diff against any version; export to Excel and Word

Review

  • Reviewer Inbox with the full review page
  • Decisions: approve, reject, challenge, request evidence, request alternative, escalate
  • Owner notified of every decision; Set Baseline on an approved version

Actuals and Reports

  • Actual hours per Estimate line with a variance cause and failed Assumption
  • Import from an Excel or CSV template with preview and confirm
  • Estimate vs actual report grouped by Integration Type, Opportunity, Product, Work Package or Cause
  • Drill-down to Opportunities and failed Assumptions, linked to the Trace
  • Pipeline and Cost and usage reports (not available yet)

Knowledge

  • Catalogue of Integration Types and Work Packages
  • Knowledge Sources tagged by product and Integration Type, with review dates, stale flag and retry
  • Checklists with mandatory items, versioned and published
  • Coverage matrix and Knowledge Graph
  • Research Agent cites Knowledge Sources in Assessments

Trust and audit

  • Append-only Trace of every human and agent action, filterable by subject, actor and event
  • Agent Registry with per-agent tool permissions; denied calls blocked and audited
  • Evals with release bars (extraction recall, citation validity, gap recall, evidence support)
  • Retention: automatic purge after the retention date, with a due list and purge history

Workspace

  • Inbox for review requests and notifications
  • Command palette (Ctrl K), keyboard shortcuts (?), collapsible sidebar
  • Light, dark or system theme; comfortable or compact density; optional single-key shortcuts
  • Roles from Auth0: presales engineer, delivery manager, engineering reviewer, Head of Delivery, sales representative, platform administrator
