## 1. Trigger and evidence (checklist 1.1 to 1.3)

**1.1 Triggering story.** There is no single triggering story. The redesign is a cross-cutting change. The stories that embody the old identity and are therefore most affected:

- **Story 1.5 (Parts A and B, `spec-1-5a-design-tokens-and-settings.md`, `spec-1-5b-app-shell.md`, both done):** tokens, shell, Settings, keyboard. Its ACs say "Inter 13px type roles", "radii 4/6/8", "density (32px or 28px rows)" and carry "Linear-style" in the title.
- **Story 1.8 (done, Parts A, B and demo slice):** workspace tabs, Overview, status pill.
- **Story 2.6 (done, two specs):** the Requirement "confirm" behaviour that the new Confirmed / Unconfirmed / Needs review vocabulary extends.
- **Stories 5.7, 6.3, 8.2, 8.7, 9.3-9.5, 9.8 and 10.1:** specify screens whose layout, actions or copy v1/v2 of the redesign change.
- **Stories 3.5, 7.5 and 10.3:** specify Coverage, Cost and Reports surfaces that the redesign restructures (Graph tab, Reports tabs, Cost relocation).

**1.2 Issue type and problem statement.** *New requirement emerged from stakeholders.* Stakeholder feedback produced a redesign that the product owner adopted wholesale (`.memlog.md`: "[ADOPTED] ... adopt the new redesign wholesale"). It (a) replaces the visual identity (dense 13px Linear-style to roomy 14px card-based), (b) restores two keyboard and viewport rules the redesign omitted, (c) renames the product to "Pre Sales Agent Framework", (d) fixes seven contrast failures, (e) declares three IN-SCOPE features with no PRD or architecture backing (Knowledge Graph, Reports Pipeline and Cost tabs, Requirement confirmation status), and (f) adds about a dozen screens and behaviours that were never specified in epics. Problem statement: the planning set (PRD, architecture, epics) and roughly 60% of the built web UI no longer match the approved UX spines, and three new feature areas lack requirements, data model and stories.

**1.3 Evidence.**

- `.memlog.md` decisions of 2026-10-21 (overrides, ADOPTED items, v2 delta folded in) and `.working/redesign-v2-delta.md` (v2 added Settings, Trace detail, Findings, Actuals, Estimate lifecycle, Challenge dialog, empty states, Offline, a11y).
- `EXPERIENCE.md` line "Scope needing PRD backing" states in its own words that Graph, Pipeline, Cost and the Requirement status have "no PRD or architecture requirement yet". Open Questions 3 to 14 are unresolved.
- `epics.md` still cites the old identity: UX-DR1 ("Inter at 13px ... tight radii (4/6/8) ... 32px or 28px rows ... activity rail 300px"), UX-DR6 ("List row component: 32px"), UX-DR10 ("running dot"), UX-DR25 (`mockups/*.html`), Story 1.5 title and ACs, 32px rows in Stories 1.7, 3.1, 3.2, 4.4, 6.1, 7.3, 9.2, 9.8, 11.1, 28px rows in 5.7.

## 3. Artifact conflicts (checklist 3.1 to 3.4)

### 3.1 PRD (`prds/prd-pre-sales-agent-2026-10-01/prd.md`, status draft, updated 2026-10-01)

No conflict with the Vision, JTBD or the success metrics. Conflicts and gaps:

1. **Requirement status vocabulary.** PRD glossary §3 defines a Requirement by classification and source link only. FR-6 says "add, edit, split, merge, delete and confirm" but defines no confirmation state. The Gap glossary entry uses "open, answered, or converted". The redesign introduces Unconfirmed / Confirmed / Needs review. Compatible with FR-6 (confirm exists) but "Needs review" and the Unconfirmed default are new. EXPERIENCE's `[ASSUMPTION]` that Confirmed is not a submit blocker is consistent with FR-14 (blocks only on Gaps and Unknowns) but no FR states it. The existing implementation already exposes "Confirmed" (check icon and label) per `spec-2-6-edit-and-confirm-requirements.md`.
2. **Knowledge Graph.** No FR. Nearest: FR-10 "A platform administrator can see which products and integration types lack documentation or Checklists", FR-8 (Knowledge Source owner, last-reviewed, stale) and FR-65 (catalogue). The graph's "Coverage score (0 to 100)", "Used by agents" shares, "Cited by", "Last retrieved" and "Stale in" have no definition anywhere in the PRD. Not a conflict with non-goals (§5), but it needs a new FR so it can be traced and tested, plus the definition of the score.
3. **Reports Pipeline tab.** FR-2 covers the Opportunity's own derived status. No FR covers a cross-Opportunity pipeline view (Opportunities by status, due soon). §5 Non-Goals say the platform is "Not a CRM, PSA or project-management tool" and the HubSpot pipeline stage must not drive actions (Q3, Story 13.3). A Pipeline report derived only from platform state (status, proposal date) does not conflict; one that mirrors CRM stages would. Needs an explicit scope sentence.
4. **Reports Cost and usage tab.** FR-59 already covers this content ("per-Workflow-Run and per-Agent traces, latency, failures, retries, token use and model cost ... Cost per Opportunity is reported"), with the viewer a "platform administrator". The redesign shows GPU seconds. Not a new capability; location and roles change (Admin → Reports, Head of Delivery). Note: `platform_model_calls` stores tokens and `latency_ms` but no GPU-seconds column (`backend/app/platform/model_gateway/models.py:41-43`); Story 7.5 already assumes "GPU time" is derived from rates in config.
5. **Estimate Version numbering and edits.** FR-35 "Every change creates a new Estimate Version" versus AD-11 and Story 8.2 (edits on a draft modify the draft; immutability starts at `submitted`) versus v2 ("Save as new version", "v3.1"). The PRD sentence is ambiguous enough to support either; a decision is needed (open question 3).
6. **Uncovered Requirement as a blocker.** The redesign lists "1 Requirement not covered" under Blockers to submit and "Covers (R-ids)" in the grid. FR-14 and AD-27 blockers do not include it; FR-33 does not require Requirement coverage on lines. Needs a decision (open question 4). The Critic already reports uncovered Requirements as coverage Findings (Story 6.4).
7. **NFR-7** says only "WCAG 2.1 AA". The redesign adds skip link, single-key shortcut switch (2.1.4), live region. Covered by UX-DR4 and UX-DR23 in epics but not in the PRD. Low impact.
8. **Name.** PRD title "Agentic AI Presales Platform" with "*Working title. Confirm.*"; spine decision: "Pre Sales Agent Framework". The code uses "Pre-Sales Agent" (`web/src/app/layout.tsx` metadata, 10 page titles).
9. **MVP.** Still achievable. No R1 goal is altered; Graph and Pipeline are additive and can sit last in R1.

### 3.2 Architecture (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md`, status final, updated 2026-10-06)

- **AD-11 and Consistency Conventions (Statuses).** Requirement carries `origin` and `locked_by_human` only; no confirmation status in the status table. Needs `confirmation_status` (`unconfirmed | confirmed | needs_review`), transitions, and the "edit of a Confirmed Requirement" rule (open deferred item in `deferred-work.md`: "Decide whether editing a confirmed Requirement should clear its confirmation").
- **No graph storage or API.** AD-1 forbids new datastores or brokers, so the Graph must be a derived read model over `knowledge_*` tables (sources, chunks, checklists, catalogue tags) in Postgres, served by a `knowledge.public` query and one REST endpoint. Fits AD-2 (`reporting_*` read-only views may join across modules) and AD-10 (the coverage score must be deterministic domain code, not LLM). "Used by agents" and "Last retrieved" need a retrieval audit: today `platform_model_calls` logs calls but not which chunks were retrieved, and Story 3.3 AC says "the query text is never logged". A retrieval log without query text (chunk ID, agent, run, time) is compatible with NFR-9 but is a new table (needs an owner in the Entity Ownership table, AD-2) and depends on Story 5.4 (ToolGateway).
- **Reports endpoints.** AD-2 and Story 10.3 provide `reporting_*` views for variance. Pipeline (Opportunities by status plus due soon) needs a status aggregation. AD-26 says Opportunity status is "derived by a query, never stored"; aggregating it across Opportunities means either a SQL view duplicating the derivation or a shared query function. Cost needs `reporting_*` views from Story 7.5 plus role-gated routes (Head of Delivery read-only).
- **Settings persistence.** The architecture is silent; the built code stores theme, density and single-key shortcuts in an httpOnly cookie `psa_prefs` (`web/src/lib/preferences.ts`, `app/settings/actions.ts`) so SSR paints the right theme. v2 stores `pa.tp`, `pa.density`, `pa.sk` in localStorage ("Saved in this browser"). No server table either way. This is an implementation divergence, not an AD conflict, but the choice (and the default for single-key shortcuts: built = on, v2 = off) should be recorded.
- **SSE and offline.** AD-16 and AD-30 are unchanged. The Offline strip and Reconnecting banner are client states derived from the existing stream (built in Story 2.3: Reconnecting bar exists).
- **Estimate Version states.** AD status list: `draft, submitted, in_review, approved, rejected, superseded`; Baseline is a pointer (`estimates_baselines`, AD-26). The v2 lifecycle stepper (Draft, In review, Approved, Baseline) and state pills (Superseded, Baseline, Draft) are a UI mapping that skips `submitted`; document the mapping. "v3.1" breaks "whole number" versions (AD-11 uses a version number); decision needed.
- **Finding vocabulary.** Epics 5.3 says severity `info | minor | major | critical`; built code and the UI use `low | medium | high | critical` (`backend/app/modules/assessments/adapters/models.py:97`); the Findings table's Type column shows "Risk, Dependency" whereas AD/5.3 kinds are `claim | unknown | assumption | risk`. Reconcile (adopt the built vocabulary in epics 5.3; add `dependency` or map it).
- **Question status.** v2 shows "Dismissed" in the question column; the spine keeps Dismissed as a Gap status (AD-23) and the question statuses `drafted, approved, sent, answered, unanswered`. UI mapping only.
- **Capability map.** Needs rows for Knowledge Graph, Pipeline report and Cost view (currently only 4.14 `reporting_*` and 4.16).

## 4. Path forward (checklist 4.1 to 4.4)

**Option 1: Direct Adjustment.** Modify planning artifacts, add Epic 17 (refit) and feature stories 2.9, 3.6, 8.9, 10.7 inside their epics, adjust ACs of unbuilt stories now, re-skin built screens incrementally. *Effort: Medium to High* (token swap Low; density, pill, component primitives and 67 files Medium; Graph and Pipeline Medium each; Reports and Cost High because the epic is unbuilt). *Risk: Medium* (UI regression across 77 test files; unresolved open questions could cause rework; Graph data definitions are new). *Viable.*

**Option 2: Rollback.** Revert done stories to re-implement in the new look. *Effort: High. Risk: High.* The done work is behavioural (APIs, domain, job queue, workflows); only the skin changes. Reverting gives no simplification and discards verified work (43 stories with done specs). *Not viable.*

**Option 3: MVP Review.** Reduce scope: drop or defer Graph, Pipeline and part of the v2 screens. *Effort: Low. Risk: Low to Medium* (stakeholders approved the redesign wholesale; deferring contradicts that decision). *Partially viable* as a sequencing tool, not as a scope cut: the PRD's R1 goal (raw input to a reviewed Estimate plus Actuals) does not depend on the Graph or Pipeline, so they can land last in R1 or slip to a follow-up release without breaking the MVP.

**Recommendation: Option 1, with Option 3 used for sequencing only.** Rationale:

1. The change is a skin plus additive features on a modular monolith whose ADs (AD-1, AD-2, AD-10, AD-26) already absorb derived read models. No rollback gains anything.
2. The cheapest moment to align is now: Epics 8 (mostly), 9, 10 and 3.4/3.5 are unbuilt, so their ACs can change at zero code cost. Every day of building on the old tokens increases refit cost.
3. Sequence the refit foundation first (Story 17.1), then keep estimate-critical stories (8.6, 9.x, 10.1) ahead of the Graph and Pipeline.
4. Gate the three PRD-less features on the PRD additions (proposals 1-3) and on the open questions below before their stories enter a sprint, as EXPERIENCE itself requires ("Each needs a PRD requirement ... before stories are written").
