# Safety and maintenance

## Emergency stops

Emergency stop circuits are tested every quarter by the vendor's service engineer. Each test is
recorded in the maintenance log with the circuit number and the result.

## Preventive maintenance

Robots are serviced every 2,000 operating hours: wheels, brushes and batteries are inspected,
and worn wheels are replaced. A service window of four hours per grid segment is needed.

## Spare parts

The site keeps a minimum stock of ten spare wheels and two spare batteries per hundred robots.
