Scope discussed
- Automated pallet warehouse with a multi-deep shuttle system for 14,000 pallet positions.
- Pallets come from three bottling and canning lines; automated guided vehicles take them
  from the line end to the warehouse infeed.
- Every pallet is labelled at the line end with an SSCC label and its batch and best-before
  date; the warehouse must ship strictly first-expiry-first-out.
- Truck loading: 40 trucks a day, pre-staged at the dock in loading sequence.

Conditions on site
- The warehouse runs at 4 to 30 degrees, unheated in winter; condensation at the doors.
- The lines' PLCs are from two different suppliers; [PERSON_3] does not have the
  communication specs for the older canning line.
- Empty pallet returns are sorted manually by a contractor and stay out of scope.

Business
- Peak in June and December, about 1,100 pallets a day out.
- The site is planning a fourth line in 2028, size not yet decided.
