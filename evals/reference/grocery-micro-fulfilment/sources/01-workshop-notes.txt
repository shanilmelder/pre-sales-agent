[CUSTOMER] - urban micro-fulfilment workshop notes
Location: [SITE] store, back-of-store area
Attendees: [PERSON_1] (vendor), [PERSON_2] (e-commerce operations), [PERSON_3] (store IT)
Note: fictional reference data for the eval set.

Goals
- Online grocery orders picked from a compact cube storage grid behind the store, with
  three pick ports, so customers can collect within one hour of ordering.
- Ambient and chilled totes in the same grid; frozen items stay in the store freezers and
  are added at the collection point.
- Target 120 orders per hour at the evening peak, average 35 items per order.

Systems
- Orders come from the e-commerce platform. Substitutions are decided by the platform, not
  by the warehouse system.
- Store stock and grid stock must be one stock figure in the merchandising system, updated
  every 10 minutes.
- [PERSON_3] is not sure the store's network can carry the grid robots' Wi-Fi alongside the
  store's handheld scanners.

Constraints
- The back-of-store area has a ceiling height of 5.2 m and a floor that has never been
  surveyed for flatness.
- Installation only at night; the store stays open 07:00-22:00.
- Noise at the collection point must stay below 70 dB.

Open
- Whether a second store will follow in the same year is not yet decided by the board.
