Aldermoor Health Trust - presales notes (Alex Chen, solutions architect)
Written after the discovery call, 22 Oct 2026, and a visit to the outpatients booking office.

Booking office
- 14 booking clerks, 2,000 calls a day, 09:00-17:00. Average call 4 min.
- Rebooking a cancelled appointment takes three screens in CarePoint. Clerks keep a paper
  list of clinics that "never accept online booking" (paediatric oncology, some
  rheumatology clinics). Those must stay clerk-only in the portal.

Clinic letters
- 60,000 letters a month, generated in CarePoint from Word templates, then printed and
  posted by an external mail house (contract ends September 2027).
- Letters for safeguarding cases must never appear in the portal. CarePoint flags these
  with a confidentiality code; we must honour it.

Referrals
- 30% of outpatient referrals arrive by fax or post and are re-keyed. Triage is by
  consultants; they want a worklist with accept / reject / redirect and a reason.
- Referral to treatment clock must start on receipt of the referral, not on triage.

Security and compliance observations
- Trust has ISO 27001 for its data centre only. They expect a penetration test before
  go-live and a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) completed with their DPO.
- Audit trail of every patient record access must be kept for 8 years.

Risks I see
- Fixed price with 99.9% 24/7 and delay damages is aggressive against GBP 1.1m.
- Go-live dates conflict between the call (April 2027) and procurement (1 February 2027).
- Patient numbers: 450,000 on the call, 600,000 in procurement's sizing.
- Hosting: "no patient data ever leaves the Trust" vs "your hosted service is fine if UK".
