Larkspur Mutual - presales notes (Alex Chen, solutions architect)
Written after the discovery call (15 Oct 2026) and a half-day with the claims team.

Claims floor
- 85 handlers in three teams: motor, home, travel. Fraud team of 12 refers suspicious
  claims to an external investigator (about 300 a year).
- Handlers re-key the policy number and cover details from the mainframe green screen
  into ClaimsOne for every claim. Average FNOL call 11 minutes.
- Storm week, Feb 2026: 4,100 home claims in five days, handlers on overtime for a month.

Reserving and finance
- Reserves are set by handlers within authority limits (EUR 5k, 25k, 100k, above that
  the claims manager). Authority limits must be enforced by the new system.
- Finance closes the month on working day 3; postings must be in D365 by 06:00.

Recoveries
- Motor recoveries from third-party insurers run on a spreadsheet today, about EUR 4m a
  year. They want it in the platform, but it wasn't in the RFP scope list.

Observations and risks
- Contradiction on volumes: 18,000 claims a month (call) vs 30,000 (procurement).
- Contradiction on go-live: motor/travel Sept 2027 and home March 2028 (call) vs all lines
  by 1 July 2027 (procurement).
- Contradiction on automation: 40% straight-through settlement (COO) vs every payment
  approved by a handler (procurement / compliance).
- Fixed price EUR 2.3m with a 10% retention and 1.4 million claims to migrate is tight.
- Regulator outsourcing review could add controls late in the project.
