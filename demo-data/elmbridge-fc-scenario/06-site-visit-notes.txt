Elmbridge fulfilment centre - site visit notes
Date: 2 October 2026
Visited by: Alex Chen (solutions engineer), with Grace Okoye (facilities) and Marcus Bell (IT)
Note: sample data for the demo; all names and companies are fictional.

Building
- 1994 steel-frame warehouse, about 18,000 m2, clear height under the haunch about 12.5 m.
- The current tenant's pallet racking covers the whole floor, so the slab could not be
  inspected. Two visible cracks near dock doors 4 and 5. Grace confirmed again that the
  slab thickness is unknown and that core samples are only possible after 15 January 2027.
- Existing ceiling sprinklers only, no in-rack sprinklers. The fire authority decision on
  in-rack sprinklers will only come after the final design review; nobody can predict it.
- Eight dock doors, all to be handed over working by the landlord.

IT room
- The legacy WMS runs on a single on-premise server under the IT desk. Marcus showed us the
  database: about 140 tables, many without clear names, and at least three scheduled jobs
  nobody could explain. He could not say which other systems read or write to it.
- The server has no test copy. Marcus confirmed the customer cannot produce an interface
  list; we will only learn the real number of interfaces by tracing them during migration.
- Wi-Fi coverage in the current building is poor at the back wall. Marcus said IT will
  provide the network, but has not decided whether the automation gets its own VLAN.

Packing area
- Current packing benches will be removed. The carton range is still open; Hannah's team
  decides it. Whether the mezzanine over docks 1 to 3 stays is also still their decision.

Follow-ups
- Send power requirements and drop positions to Grace, so they can decide who does the supply.
- Send network requirements and Wi-Fi coverage plan to Marcus.
- Price separate allowances for: legacy WMS interfaces, floor slab, sprinkler redesign,
  product data clean-up, marketplace volume headroom.
