# Harbourline Home & Garden - Elmbridge FC Tender Specification

Version 2.0, issued 2 October 2026 by Harbourline Procurement. Requirements marked M are mandatory, D desirable.

## 1. Throughput and capacity

| ID | Requirement | M/D |
|----|-------------|-----|
| TP-01 | Peak hour throughput of 6,000 order lines in Black Friday week | M |
| TP-02 | AS/RS capacity for 60,000 totes, with 20% free locations at peak | M |
| TP-03 | Goods-to-person ports at 450 picks per hour per port | M |
| TP-04 | Same-day despatch for orders placed before 20:00 | M |
| TP-05 | Bulky items (over 30 kg or 1.2 m) handled outside the automation | M |

## 2. Software and integration

- Replacement of the 2009 in-house WMS by the supplier's WMS, integrated with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance and Operations for item master, purchase orders, sales orders and stock adjustments.
- Real-time stock visibility in Dynamics 365 within 2 minutes of a movement.
- Two parcel carriers (to be chosen in Q1 2027): labels, manifests and tracking events.
- Staff single sign-on.
- Dashboards for throughput, port utilisation and order backlog, visible on the warehouse floor.

## 3. Non-functional

- Availability 99.7% during operating hours (06:00-24:00, 7 days).
- Recovery from a WMS server failure within 30 minutes.
- Audit trail of stock adjustments kept for 7 years.

## 4. Programme

- Contract award mid-December 2026; site access from 15 January 2027.
- Go-live by 1 August 2027, so peak trials can run in September.
- Training for 160 operators and 12 super-users, including a night shift.

## 5. Commercial

- Fixed price including named allowances for unknowns, each with its hours.
- Liquidated damages of GBP 10,000 per day of delay to go-live.
- Spare parts held on site for the first two years.
